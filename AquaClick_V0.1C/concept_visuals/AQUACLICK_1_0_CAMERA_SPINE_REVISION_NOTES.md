# AquaClick 1.0 Camera And Spine Revision Notes

Generated output:
- `AquaClick_V0.1C/concept_visuals/aquaclick_1_0_half_height_spine_camera_angle.png`

Revision decisions:
- The dry electronics spine should not be full hopper height for V1.0 unless a large battery, tall speaker chamber, or stacked module ecosystem becomes a confirmed requirement.
- Current direction uses a half-height to mid-height dry spine, visually subordinate to the washable food cassette.
- Camera belongs to the dry spine, not to the food hopper, chute, rotor, tray, or bowl.
- Camera position is the upper front corner of the dry spine, aimed diagonally down and left toward the bowl, food outlet, and cat eating position.
- This position preserves the core product claim: the dirty food path remains circuit-free and removable for washing or replacement.

Engineering implications:
- Camera FOV should be validated with a simple mockup at bowl-mouth height before industrial design freeze.
- Target camera height should likely sit above the bowl rim but below the hopper lid line.
- The camera module needs an angled housing or wedge so the lens can see the cat face/head and bowl without requiring the spine to be full height.
- Module slot layout should avoid implying that the food cassette contains electronics.

Image status:
- This is a concept visualisation for product direction and supplier conversation.
- It is not a manufacturable CAD model, mold drawing, tolerance drawing, or validated optical package.
