# AquaClick 1.0 Form Concept Notes

Label: 概念效果圖 / Concept visualisation

Image:

- `aquaclick_1_0_washable_cassette_form_concept.png`

## Keep

- Transparent removable food cassette as the visual hero.
- Clear wet/dry separation seam between cassette and dry base.
- Front mechanical latch / Click release as a visible interaction point.
- Matte warm-white dry base, transparent smoke-tinted cassette, pale aqua accent.
- Simple stainless bowl and removable chute path.
- Friendly home-appliance proportions, not sci-fi gadget styling.

## Rework Before Engineering CAD

- Cassette lift path and handle ergonomics.
- Real latch geometry, spring, retention face and release force.
- Gasket/labyrinth boundary between washable cassette and dry base.
- Actual motor-to-rotor coupling package.
- Food outlet geometry and residue-free chute liner.
- Bowl removal and load-cell force path if weighing remains in scope.

## Do Not Treat As Validated

- Not a production design.
- Not a mold-ready surface.
- Not proof of cleanability, anti-jam performance, waterproofing, or food-contact compliance.
- Not confirmation that the shown wall thickness, seams, or latch positions are manufacturable.
